Bio

New Orleans based composer

I received a B.A. in Music from UC Berkeley where I studied digital signal processing and composition. I play the tuba. I have the insight now of having worked in a church, providing music literally as a "service".

I quote Elvis in Love Me (Long) and give away the bit at the end. With "Love Me (Long)," I experimented with creating a gradient of dissonance to consonance. I wrote Astrocasper for the Walden summer music festival in New Hampshire. First, I lull the audience into a noisy romantic daydream. Then I break the spell with theater and irreverent quotations of Radiohead and J-Dilla. Siddhartha was a community I had to build. In my opera Siddhartha, I worked with an ensemble of dancers and singers, professionals and amateurs, to develop the vocal and movement material for the second act. In Film B, I use computation in the notation domain: generate score for players, render score from players.

I'm investigating how to use a team of professionals to kickstart a community of musicians that sustains itself. How can art bring people together to make more art that brings people together to make art?
