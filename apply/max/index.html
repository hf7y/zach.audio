The proposal

What I'm proposing is that I will host weekly open performances where masters at the performances reproduce (mediated by notation) the previous week's performance including the community performers who attended. The goal of year five is that the communication has been modified without me.

The thesis

What music persuades its recipients to become senders? The object of study is the anthem which is communication of an identity through performance. The recipients of that identity are persuaded to perform a social function through the motivation of identification. The performance of that social function demands further musical use. I have rounded that loop many times already, though it has never outlived me. I have two examples of that loop returning with an artifact (performance or recording), but it gets blocked on starting the next cycle. I'm going to land in a place. My work is going to be written for professional musicians (of whatever ensemble the school has to work with). Then those musicians with me are going to play for an audience not of listeners, but amateur performers, who will identify with the work and also be able to adapt it. The loop closes on itself when I can exit. The works I'm creating are anthems, or charters, essentially founding documents which lay out the development of the self-perpetuating performance community. The performance of the anthem models the community's particular guardrails around adaptation. The ensemble closed the loop without me and we can study the output: how has it been adapted? why? The final portfolion includes, anthems I've written, along with works composed in the course of looping up until the moment of the defense.

The mechanism

The idea is to create works that come alive and continue development out of my hands. It's an ethic. It creates a micro-polity. In the parade, my abstention is legible. I can refuse to join a chant, crucially. With the pressing of the music into a record, compression reduces the dynamic range, making my abstention inaudible, my silence is silenced. The composer (the musical "user") adapts absolute (Dahlhaus) stimulation and programmed associations to produce a message. That message persuades recipients to effect changes that reinforce the social function that produces similar messages. The loop is closed by identification as members of the group reenact the ritual while performing the shared identity. The loop develops through adaptation as the performance evolves, shifting the identity it communicates which changes the effect on recipients. What music do individuals in their freedom choose to reproduce, and why?

The compositions as evidence

Love Me (Long)

Score

Nicolee Kuester, Daniel Lippel, Jacqueline Kerrod

This piece was inspired by my grandmother who loves Elvis. I have fond memories sitting with her at the piano bench singing and playing songs when I was very young. With "Love Me (Long)," I experimented with creating a gradient of dissonance to consonance. I was surprised to discover the effect this had on me as a listener yearning for the hidden beauty to emerge.

Love Me has the best harmonic ideas.
The form of Love Me is the most sophisticated.

Astrocasper

Score

Mivos Quartet, Matthew Gold

I wrote Astrocasper for the Walden summer music festival in New Hampshire. "Casper" refers to the harmonies of Ravel's Gaspard de la nuit. First, I lull the audience into a noisy romantic daydream. Then I break the spell with theater and irreverent quotations of Radiohead and J-Dilla.

Astrocasper has the score literally disappear until it's just lines or blank notes.
