The thesis

professionals teach amateurs the anthem, then amateurs meet and play the anthem and draft the future music with me. then I exit the loop when notation is produces without my hand once

The proposed research

I'm investigating how to use a team of professionals to kickstart a community of musicians that sustains itself.

What Ken speaks to

Two compositions in my portfolio demonstrate how I have composed in a group-specific way. In my opera Siddhartha, I worked with an ensemble of dancers and singers, professionals and amateurs, to develop the vocal and movement material for the second act. In my score for "By the time I see you in the morning", I worked with a mixed group of classical and jazz string players to produce an extended timbral palette, combining notation and verbal instruction to suit the players.
