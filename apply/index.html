The proposal

The model is a blend of devised work and notated instruction. The workshop period begins with a score played by a professional ensemble. The community works with the ensemble to develop that core material, the reaction of the community players is fed back mediated by notation, which is a software pipeline I refine that has human quality control, this runs in a measured period of rehearsals culminating in a public performance. I repeat that structure after a period of integration and refinement. The goal is to develop a system that I can cleanly hand off to community members who have become stakeholders, trained on the approach, and with a motivation model that sustains the loop.

The thesis

My goal is to develop a model for a performance community that develops its own material and reproduces itself.

The first two semesters

The first semester tests the recording to score pipeline with me in the loop. I hand build the scores while aligning the mechanism to reproduce the work. The next semester I hand off the scoring and mechanism, training others to run it.

Works

Love Me (Long)

Score Recording Video

Performers: Nicolee Kuester, Daniel Lippel, Jacqueline Kerrod

This piece was inspired by my grandmother who loves Elvis. I have fond memories sitting with her at the piano bench singing and playing songs when I was very young. With "Love Me (Long)," I experimented with creating a gradient of dissonance to consonance. I was surprised to discover the effect this had on me as a listener yearning for the hidden beauty to emerge.

Love Me has the best harmonic ideas.
The form of Love Me is the most sophisticated.

Astrocasper

Score Recording

Performers: Mivos Quartet, Matthew Gold

I wrote Astrocasper for the Walden summer music festival in New Hampshire. "Casper" refers to the harmonies of Ravel's Gaspard de la nuit. First, I lull the audience into a noisy romantic daydream. Then I break the spell with theater and irreverent quotations of Radiohead and J-Dilla.

Astrocasper has the score literally disappear until it's just lines or blank notes.
