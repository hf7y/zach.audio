The proposal

I'm investigating how to use a team of professionals to kickstart a community of musicians that sustains itself.

The model is a blend of devised work and notated instruction. The workshop period begins with a score played by a professional ensemble.

The goal is to develop a system that I can cleanly hand off to community members who have become stakeholders, trained on the approach, and with a motivation model that sustains the loop.

The thesis

professionals teach amateurs the anthem, then amateurs meet and play the anthem and draft the future music with me. then I exit the loop when notation is produces without my hand once

The model

The model is the anthem, prime reference is Anthem for Eris. Anthem embodies the ethos of the community just like Ken's person-specific material does. In this case the community-specific writing is for the decision making model represented by varying degrees of agency (adaptation) granted to the community members.

They take on the identity of being "in" the thing. But being "in" Eris is slippery. It's unsanctioned, unpermitted, anyone can show up and play along. There are no floats. The attendees dress to the theme to demonstrate their inside knowledge, so the second line (the walkers behind the band) have the identity too (they know the bit, got an "invitation" and exulisve bit of hand made ephemera showing the theme)

The old guard argues amongst itself

The first two semesters

The first semester tests the recording to score pipeline with me in the loop. I hand build the scores while aligning the mechanism to reproduce the work. The next semester I hand off the scoring and mechanism, training others to run it.
