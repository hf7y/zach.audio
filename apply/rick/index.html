The thesis

What music persuades its recipients to become senders? The idea is to create works that come alive and continue development out of my hands. It's an ethic. It creates a micro-polity. In the parade, my abstention is legible. I can refuse to join a chant, crucially. With the pressing of the music into a record, compression reduces the dynamic range, making my abstention inaudible, my silence is silenced. The composer (the musical "user") adapts absolute (Dahlhaus) stimulation and programmed associations to produce a message. That message persuades recipients to effect changes that reinforce the social function that produces similar messages. The loop is closed by identification as members of the group reenact the ritual while performing the shared identity. The loop develops through adaptation as the performance evolves, shifting the identity it communicates which changes the effect on recipients. What music do individuals in their freedom choose to reproduce, and why?
