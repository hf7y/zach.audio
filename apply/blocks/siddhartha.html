I set Siddhartha, based on Herman Hesses' orientalist original, in a Christian church, something I know nothing about; it's a reverse orientalism, occidentalism?, where I make similar foreignisms as Hesse does but about a different culture, a hegemonic one.
In my opera Siddhartha, I worked with an ensemble of dancers and singers, professionals and amateurs, to develop the vocal and movement material for the second act.
In Siddhartha, I represented the philosophies of the characters journey through the compositional discipline associated with each: "Total Abstration" for example.
