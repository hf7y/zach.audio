Chiasmus, transcribed for the Krewe of Eris

Score: B♭ clarinet part, 2 pp. (PDF) Listen: Krewe of Eris, 2015 (9:39)

In "Chiasmus" transcribed for Eris, I wrote out the secret Latin choral part, but I whited out the noteheads and the lyrics. The material is still spaced to fit, but learning the music is left as an exercise for the player. You need to go to rehearsal and learn it socially.
